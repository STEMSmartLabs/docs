    Assemble the Tail and Decorate the Robot Dog¶

    In this step, we will attach the tail to the Robot Dog and then decorate the face creatively. This is the step that gives the Robot Dog its personality.

    The tail is assembled to the slot at the back of the robot dog. After that, you can use sketch pens, markers, crayons, or colour pencils to draw the eyes, nose, mouth, ears, and other creative details.

    Parts Required¶

    Part Quantity Description
    Tail cardboard part 1 Cardboard tail piece of the Robot Dog
    Colour pens / markers As needed Used to decorate the Robot Dog face
    Pencil 1 Optional, for drawing the face outline before colouring
    Eraser 1 Optional, for correcting the pencil sketch

    Tail Part¶

    The image below shows the Robot Dog and the tail part.

    1. Identify the Tail Slot¶

    Look at the back side of the Robot Dog. You will see a slot where the tail can be assembled.

    2. Assemble the Tail¶

    Take the cardboard tail piece and assemble it into the tail slot

    3. Adjust the Tail Position¶

    After inserting the tail, adjust it so that it stands upright.

    Tail Assembled on Robot Dog¶

    The image below shows the tail assembled on the Robot dog.

    4. Decorate the Robot Dog Face¶

    Now bring out your creativity and decorate the Robot Dog face.

    You can draw:

    • Two eyes
    • Nose
    • Mouth
    • Tongue
    • Ears
    • Eyebrows
    • Cheeks
    • Spots or patterns
    • Name of your Robot Dog

    Use a pencil first if you want to sketch lightly before colouring.

    5. Creative Face Ideas¶

    Here are some fun ideas:

    Style What to Draw
    Happy Puppy Big eyes, smiling mouth, tongue sticking out
    Super Robot Dog Sharp eyes, lightning mark, bold outline
    Friendly Pet Round eyes, small nose, cute smile
    Space Dog Stars, antenna, space helmet style lines
    Police Dog Badge symbol, serious eyes, collar
    Cartoon Dog Large eyes, colourful ears, funny expression

    6. Colouring Tips¶

    Use light colours first and then add darker outlines.

    Suggested colours:

    Area Suggested Colour
    Eyes Black, brown, or blue
    Nose Black or dark brown
    Mouth Black or red
    Tongue Red or pink
    Ears Brown, orange, pink, or any colour you like
    Face outline Black marker
    Body patterns Spots, stripes, or your own design

    7. Add Your Robot Dog Name¶

    You can also give your Robot Dog a name.

    Example names:

    • Robo
    • Bolt
    • Bingo
    • Spark
    • Bruno
    • Pixel
    • Chitti
    • Buddy

    Write the name on the side of the body or near the head.

    Troubleshooting¶

    Issue Possible Reason Solution
    Tail is loose Tail is not inserted fully Push it gently into the slot
    Tail bends Too much force was applied Remove and reinsert carefully
    Tail touches servo wire Wire is routed too close to the tail Move the wire to the side
    Back legs hit the tail Tail is inserted too low or tilted Adjust the tail position
    Face drawing smudges Ink has not dried Allow the marker ink to dry fully

    Your DIY Robot Dog is ready for final assembly
