    Robot Dog Trunk Assembly¶

    In this step, we will make the trunk (body) of the Robot Dog from the pre-cut cardboard and insert the servo motor horn assembly prepared in the previous step. The trunk supports the head and tail mechanism, so fold it carefully and keep the servo wires free.

    Parts Required¶

    Part Quantity Purpose
    Trunk cardboard piece 1 Pre-cut main body with marked fold lines and a rectangular servo opening
    Servo motor horn assembly 1 Servo motor with its previously assembled cardboard connector and horn

    1. Identify the Trunk Cardboard¶

    Take the trunk cardboard piece shown below. Find the marked fold lines, the rectangular opening in the centre, and the small fixing holes.

    Before folding, check that the cardboard is not damaged and that the rectangular opening is clear.

    2. Fold the Trunk on All Sides¶

    1. Place the trunk cardboard on a flat surface.
    2. Gently fold along each marked line, working around all the sides of the cardboard.
    3. Raise the side panels and fold the end flaps inward to form the trunk shape, as demonstrated in the assembly video.
    4. Keep the folds straight so that the panels fit together neatly.
    5. Make sure the rectangular opening remains accessible for the servo motor.

    Tip

    Fold a little at a time along the scored lines. Avoid bending the cardboard at an unmarked position, as it may tear.

    3. Take the Prepared Servo Motor Horn¶

    Use the servo motor horn assembly completed in the previous step. The picture below shows the servo attached to its cardboard connector.

    Before fitting the assembly:

    • Confirm that the servo horn and cardboard connector are securely attached.
    • Keep the servo motor powered OFF.
    • If the servo has already been set to its starting position, do not rotate its shaft or horn by hand.

    4. Insert the Servo Motor Horn Assembly at the Top¶

    1. Locate the rectangular opening in the trunk cardboard.
    2. Position the prepared servo motor assembly at the top of the trunk, as shown in the reference image.
    3. Carefully guide the servo motor into the opening and seat the horn connector in its intended position.
    4. Adjust the folded cardboard panels around the assembly so that it fits neatly, without forcing the servo or crushing the cardboard.
    5. Route the servo wire through the open space so that it is not pinched between the folded panels.

    The following image shows the trunk with the servo motor inserted and the wire routed outside the opening.

    Warning

    Do not pull on the servo cable or force the servo into the cutout. Check its orientation against the assembly image before pressing it into place.

    5. Check the Assembly¶

    Before moving on, verify that:

    • All sides of the trunk are folded along their marked lines.
    • The trunk holds its intended shape.
    • The servo motor horn assembly is positioned at the top.
    • The servo motor fits into the rectangular opening.
    • The servo wire is free and is not trapped under the cardboard.
    • The horn and connector remain firmly attached.

    Completed Step¶

    The Robot Dog trunk is now folded, and the servo motor horn assembly is in position.
