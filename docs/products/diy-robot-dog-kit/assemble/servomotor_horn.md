
## 1. Servo Motor Horn Setup

In this step, we will prepare the two servo motor horns used for the Robot Dog's front and back leg. Each servo motor must be fitted with a servo horn in the correct center position before assembling the front and back legs.

Use **two identical sets** of the parts shown below to create two servo horn assemblies.

<img src="../images/Servo_Motors_Horn_Setup_Parts.jpeg" alt="Servo motor horn setup parts" width="100%">

## Parts Required

| Qty | Part | Use |
|---:|---|---|
| 2 | Servo motors | Used to move the front and back leg mechanisms. |
| 2 | Servo horn sets | Used as the rotating arm attached to each servo motor shaft. |
| 2 | Small servo screws | Used to lock the servo horn onto the servo motor shaft. |
| 4 | Medium servo screws | Used to lock the servo onto the icecream stick. |
| 2 | Ice cream sticks | Used later as support/link parts during leg assembly. |


## Step-by-Step Instructions

### 1. Identify the Servo Motor Parts

Take one servo motor set and identify the following:

- Servo motor
- Servo horn
- Small screw for fixing the horn
- Two screws for fixing the servo onto the icecream stick
- Ice cream stick

Repeat the same for the second servo motor set.

### 2. Place the Servo Horn on the Shaft

Fit the servo horn on top of the servo motor shaft. Align it as shown in the assembly video.

Make sure:

- The horn sits properly on the servo shaft.
- The horn is not tilted.
- The horn is aligned before tightening the screw.

### 3. Fix the Horn Using the Small Screw

Insert the small screw into the center of the horn and tighten it gently.

Do not over-tighten the screw. Tighten only enough so that the horn does not come loose during movement.

### 4. Repeat for the Second Servo Motor

Use the second servo motor set and repeat the same process.

At the end of this step, you should have:

- One servo motor with horn fixed for the front leg mechanism.
- One servo motor with horn fixed for the back leg mechanism.

## Final Check

Before moving to the next assembly step, check the following:

| Check | Expected Result |
|---|---|
| Both servos have horns attached | Yes |
| Horn screws are fixed | Yes |
| Servo wires are not damaged | Yes |
| Horns are aligned as shown in the video | Yes |

## Common Mistakes to Avoid

| Mistake | How to Avoid It |
|---|---|
| Fixing the horn before centering the servo | First calibrate the servo to **initial position** as explained in the brain controller section, then attach the horn. |
| Over-tightening the screw | Tighten gently until the horn is secure. |
| Forcing the servo shaft by hand | Use the program provided in brain controller section to initialize the servo position. |
