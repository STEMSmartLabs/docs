# Robot Dog Top Assembly

Let's build the head and tail section of your Robot Dog! Before assembling anything, lay out the parts and match them with the numbers in the picture.

## Parts Identification
| No. | Part | Description and purpose |
|:---:|---|---|
| **1** | **Trunk** | The trunk cardboard piece with fold lines, mounting holes, and a rectangular opening. It forms the main folded structure that supports the head-and-tail assembly. |
| **2** | **Neck** | Neck cardboard piece with several holes and marked fold lines. |
| **3** | **Tail** | Tail narrow strip with two visible mounting holes.|
| **4** | **Servo Motor Connector** | Rounded-rectangle pieces for connecting the Servo Motor. These cardboard sections need to be joined to head and tail and will control the movement. |
| **5** | **Screws, nuts, and plastic fasteners** | Metal screws and nuts, plus white plastic fasteners. Use the appropriate pieces to secure the cardboard parts and any moving connections. |
| **6** | **Robot Dog face** | The dog-shaped front face panel with ears, together with two small round cardboard pieces. These give the Robot Dog its face and provide small mounting or spacer pieces for assembly. |
| **7** | **Micro:bit with expansion board** | The electronic controller that runs the program and sends signals to connected components. |
| **8** | **Servo motor and servo horns** | The small blue servo motor, its lead, and the white plastic horn attachments. The servo provides controlled movement to the mechanism. |

!!! tip "Before you begin"
    Keep all the screws, nuts, servo horns, and small cardboard pieces in an order.

## Prepare for assembly

1. **Check the cardboard pieces.** Identify all pre-cut holes, slots, and fold lines. Do not punch extra holes unless the assembly instructions require them.
2. **Keep the electronics powered OFF.** Do not connect or power the micro:bit while tightening the cardboard hardware.

## Final checklist

- All eight numbered part groups have been identified.
- All fixed screws and nuts are secure.
