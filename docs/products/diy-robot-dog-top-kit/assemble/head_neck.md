
# Attach the Robot Dog Head to the Neck

Now that the **Robot Dog head** and **neck mechanism** are assembled, it is time to join them together. We will use the **top screw already fixed to the forehead** and the **top hole in the neck cardboard piece**.

## Parts Required

| Part | Quantity | Purpose |
|---|---:|---|
| Assembled Robot Dog head | 1 | The head prepared in the previous step |
| Robot Dog body with neck attached | 1 | Supports the head and connects it to the servo mechanism |
| Forehead screw | 1 | Already attached to the folded forehead of the head |
| Nut | 1 | Secures the forehead screw to the neck |

## Before You Start

- Make sure the head is fully assembled, including the two round cardboard pieces and the lower white screw.
- Check that the **metal screw on the forehead** is in place.
- Locate the **top hole** on the long neck cardboard piece.
- Turn **OFF** the expansion board before working on the assembly.

## Step 1: Identify the Forehead Screw

Take the assembled head. Find the metal screw fitted through the folded cardboard tab in the middle of the forehead, between the two ears.

<img src="../images/04_RobotDog_Head.jpeg" alt="Assembled Robot Dog head with forehead screw" width="70%">

## Step 2: Align the Head with the Neck

Hold the head in front of the Robot Dog body, with its face looking forward.

1. Bring the folded forehead tab towards the **top of the neck**.
2. Line up the **forehead screw** with the **top hole in the neck cardboard**.
3. Pass the existing screw through the neck hole carefully.

**Do not make a new hole:** use the top hole already provided in the neck piece.

## Step 3: Secure the Head

1. Place **one nut** onto the screw on the opposite side of the neck.
2. Tighten the nut gently until the head is securely attached.
3. Check that the head is centred and facing forward.

!!! caution
    Do not overtighten the nut or crush the cardboard. Keep the connection secure without restricting the intended movement of the head mechanism.

## Step 4: Check the Finished Assembly

Your Robot Dog should now look like the assembled model below.

<img src="../images/05_RobotDog_Head_Neck.jpeg" alt="Robot Dog head attached to the neck and trunk" width="70%">

Check the following:

- The **forehead screw** passes through the **top neck hole**.
- The nut is fitted and the head does not wobble loosely.
- The head faces forward and sits evenly on the body.
- The head and neck have clearance to move without catching on the cardboard trunk.
- Servo wires are free and are not trapped by the head or neck.

**Do not force the servo shaft or head by hand.** Once the wiring and program are ready, test the head movement through the servo controller.
