# Servo Motor Horn Setup

In this step, we will assemble the **servo motor connector** and attach the **servo horn** to the servo motor. This mechanism connects the servo motor to the cardboard so that the robot can move it.

## Parts Required

<img src="../images/Servo_Motor_Setup.jpeg" alt="Servo motor connector cardboard pieces, screws and nuts, servo motor, servo horn, and small screws" width="80%">

| Part | Quantity | Purpose |
|---|---:|---|
| Servo motor connector cardboard pieces | 2 | Rounded rectangular cardboard pieces that form the connector for the head or tail |
| Screws and nuts | 2 sets | Secure the folded cardboard connector |
| Servo motor | 1 | Produces movement for the head or tail mechanism |
| White plastic servo horn | 1 | Transfers movement from the servo motor to the cardboard connector |
| Small horn-mounting screws | 3 | Fasten the servo horn to the cardboard connector |

> **Before starting:** If you are using the servo-centering program, set the servo to its **90° initial position** first and turn OFF the expansion board. Avoid rotating the servo shaft after centering it.

## Step 1: Fold the Servo Motor Connector Cardboard

1. Take the rounded-rectangle **servo motor connector** cardboard pieces shown above.
2. Fold the connector along its marked fold line, following the shape demonstrated in the assembly video.
3. Bring the ends together and align the screw holes.
4. Insert **2 screws** at the end of the folded cardboard and secure them with **2 nuts**.
5. Tighten gently until the connector holds its shape. Do not crush the cardboard.

## Step 2: Fix the Servo Horn to the Cardboard Connector

1. Take the **white plastic servo horn** supplied with the servo motor.
2. Position the horn on the cardboard connector, aligning its mounting holes with the prepared holes in the cardboard.
3. Insert **2 small screws** near the ends of the servo horn to fasten the horn to the connector.
4. Check that the horn is firmly attached and sits straight.

## Step 3: Attach the Connector to the Servo Motor

1. Take the **servo motor** and orient its shaft toward the attached horn.
2. Carefully **push the servo motor into the folded cardboard connector**, as demonstrated in the video.
3. Align the center of the horn and connector with the servo motor shaft. Press the horn onto the shaft without forcing it.
4. Take the **remaining small servo screw** and insert it through the **middle of the cardboard/horn assembly** into the center of the servo motor shaft.
5. Tighten the center screw gently so the cardboard connector and horn remain fixed to the servo motor.

!!! warning "Do not force the servo motor"
    Do not twist the servo shaft by hand or overtighten the screws. Keep the servo cable clear of the moving connector.

## Step 4: Check the Assembly

- The cardboard connector is folded neatly.
- Both connector screws and nuts are secure.
- The horn is fixed to the cardboard with its **2 small screws**.
- The **center screw** holds the horn onto the servo motor shaft.
- The cardboard connector is straight and does not rub against the servo housing.
- The servo wire is free and not pinched.
