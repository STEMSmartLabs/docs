# Robot Dog Head Assembly

Let's assemble the Robot Dog's head! In this step, you will attach the two round cardboard pieces, secure the lower part of the face, and fold the forehead into position.

## Parts Required

<img src="../images/RobotDog_Head.jpeg" alt="Robot Dog head cardboard parts, circular pieces, white screws, and a metal screw with nut" 
  style="width: 75%; height: 320px; object-fit: contain; background: #f5f5f5; border-radius: 8px;">

| Part | Quantity | Purpose |
|---|---:|---|
| Robot Dog head cardboard | 1 | Main face, ears, and foldable forehead |
| Round cardboard pieces | 2 | Reinforcement pieces on either side of the face |
| White screws | 3 | Two to attach the round pieces and one for the bottom of the face |
| Metal screw and nut | 1 set | Secures the folded forehead |

## Assembly Instructions

### Step 1: Identify the head parts

Place the head cardboard on a flat surface with the ears pointing upward. Find the **three holes on the face** and the **hole on the forehead flap**. Keep the two round cardboard pieces and all fasteners nearby.

### Step 2: Fix the round cardboard pieces

1. Place one round cardboard piece over the hole on the **left side** of the face.
2. Align its center hole with the hole in the head cardboard.
3. Insert **one white screw** to secure it.
4. Repeat on the **right side** with the second round cardboard piece and another white screw.

Make sure both round pieces are aligned and sit flat against the cardboard.

### Step 3: Insert the bottom white screw

Insert the **third white screw** through the hole at the **bottom center of the face**. Press it into place gently so it is secure.

### Step 4: Fold and secure the forehead

1. Locate the fold line on the **forehead flap**, between the two ears.
2. Gently fold the forehead cardboard along the marked line.
3. Align the forehead hole with the matching fixing point.
4. Insert the **metal screw** and tighten it with the **nut** to hold the folded forehead in position.

Do not overtighten the metal screw, as it may damage the cardboard.

## Completed Head Assembly

<img src="../images/04_RobotDog_Head.jpeg" alt="Completed Robot Dog head with two round cardboard pieces, three white screws, and folded forehead secured with a metal screw" 
  style="width: 75%; height: 320px; object-fit: contain; background: #f5f5f5; border-radius: 8px;">

Your Robot Dog head is ready! Compare your assembled piece with the example above.

## Final Checklist

- Both round cardboard pieces are secured with **one white screw each**.
- The **third white screw** is fixed at the bottom of the face.
- The forehead flap is folded along its marked line.
- The forehead is secured using **one metal screw and one nut**.
- All fasteners are snug, and the cardboard is not bent or crushed.

!!! tip "Assembly tip"
    Fold the cardboard slowly along the marked line. If the holes do not line up, adjust the fold before tightening the screw rather than forcing the cardboard.
