
## Robot Unicorn Experiment

Experiment building the Robot Unicorn head and replace the Robot Dog head.

Let's give your robot a magical unicorn head! In this step, you will fold the cardboard head, secure its shape, attach the two round side pieces, and connect the finished head to the robot's neck.

## Parts Required

<img src="../images/RobotUnicorn_Head_Parts.jpeg" alt="Unicorn head cardboard, two round cardboard pieces, screws, nuts, and white plastic screws" 
  style="width: 75%; height: 320px; object-fit: contain; background: #f5f5f5; border-radius: 8px;">

| Part | Quantity | Purpose |
|---|---:|---|
| Unicorn head cardboard | 1 | Forms the face, ears, and unicorn horn |
| Round cardboard pieces | 2 | Attach to both sides of the face |
| Metal screws | 3 | Two to hold the bottom of the folded head and one to attach the head to the neck |
| Metal nuts | 3 | Secure the three metal screws |
| White plastic screws | 2 | Fix the two round cardboard pieces |
| Assembled robot body with neck | 1 | Supports the completed unicorn head |

!!! tip "Before you start"
    Identify the fold lines and all the holes in the head cardboard. Keep the robot switched **OFF** while assembling.

## Step 1: Fold the Unicorn Head

1. Place the unicorn head cardboard on a flat surface.
2. Gently fold it along the **pre-marked lines** to form the head.
3. Bring the side sections into position so the head takes shape.
4. Keep the **ears and pointed unicorn horn** upright.

Avoid forcing the cardboard. Fold slowly to prevent tearing along the crease lines.

## Step 2: Secure the Bottom of the Head

1. Align the holes in the folded cardboard at the **bottom of the head**.
2. Insert **two metal screws**, one through each matching bottom fixing point.
3. Attach **one nut to each screw**.
4. Tighten gently until the folded head holds its shape.

**Used in this step:** 2 metal screws + 2 nuts.

## Step 3: Attach the Two Round Cardboard Pieces

1. Take the **two round cardboard pieces**.
2. Place one on each side of the unicorn's face, aligned with the side holes.
3. Insert a **white plastic screw** through each round piece and its matching hole.
4. Press the plastic screws into place so both round pieces sit securely.

**Used in this step:** 2 round pieces + 2 white plastic screws.

!!! note
    Both round pieces should be fitted evenly on opposite sides of the face, as shown in the reference image.

## Step 4: Fold the Top Tab Between the Ears

1. Locate the small cardboard tab **between the unicorn's ears**, beneath the pointed horn.
2. Fold the tab along its marked line.
3. Insert the **remaining metal screw** through the hole in this folded top tab.
4. Leave the screw ready to connect to the robot's neck.

**Used in this step:** 1 metal screw. Keep the last nut for the next step.

## Step 5: Connect the Unicorn Head to the Neck

1. Hold the completed unicorn head in front of the robot's neck.
2. Align the screw in the **top folded tab** with the **top hole of the neck**.
3. Pass the screw through the neck hole.
4. Fasten it using the **remaining nut**.
5. Check that the unicorn head faces forward and is attached securely.

**Used in this step:** 1 nut.

## Completed Unicorn Head

<img src="../images/06_RobotUnicorn_Head.jpeg" alt="Completed assembled Robot Unicorn head" 
  style="width: 75%; height: 320px; object-fit: contain; background: #f5f5f5; border-radius: 8px;">

Your robot now has a unicorn head with two ears and a pointed horn!

## Final Assembly Checklist

- Cardboard is folded neatly along the marked lines.
- Two screws and nuts hold the bottom of the head together.
- The two round side pieces are fixed with white plastic screws.
- The tab between the ears is folded correctly.
- The top screw passes through the neck hole and is secured with the final nut.
- The head is aligned and securely attached.
- The head and neck can move without the cardboard snagging or servo wires getting pulled.

!!! warning "Handle with care"
    Do not overtighten the metal screws or force the cardboard folds. After connecting the head, check its movement gently before turning the robot on. Keep fingers clear of moving parts.

## What's Next?

Once the head is securely attached, continue with the remaining robot assembly and testing steps. You can also **draw eyes, a smile, and colourful details** to give your Robot Unicorn its own personality!
